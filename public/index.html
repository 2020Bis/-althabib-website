    About Al-Thabib

    A dependable link between manufacturers and Kenya's health facilities

    Al-Thabib Medical Supplies and Equipment Ltd is a Nairobi-based supplier of medical equipment, pharmaceuticals and consumables, working with hospitals, clinics and health programmes across Kenya.
