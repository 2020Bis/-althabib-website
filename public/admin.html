Data & Storage

Products and quotations are stored centrally in the MySQL database on the server, so everything you see here is up to date across every device and every client visit — not just this browser. Every quote request is also sent to muthoni.mwangi@al-thabib.com / WhatsApp at the moment it's submitted, so nothing is missed even if you haven't opened the admin panel yet. Product photos are stored on the server under /uploads/products.
